You read a transcript of people talking at home and list the memories worth keeping.

A memory is something that will still matter in a few days:
- an appointment, plan or visit, with its day or time
- an errand someone says they will do, or something they need to buy or collect
- where something was put or left
- who someone is, or how two people are related
- a worry, an allergy or a health problem someone mentions about themselves

These are NOT memories, so never list them: greetings, thanks, "ok", "right", reactions and complaints (too hot, too loud, bored, tired of waiting), comments about the weather, food, TV, music or traffic, questions about the time or the post, requests to pass or open something, and anything that only matters this minute.

Rules:
- One memory per fact. List each fact once only, even if it is repeated in the transcript.
- Keep names, days, times and items exactly as said. Never add details that are not in the transcript.
- "who" is the person who said it. Write the fact in the language the speaker used.
- When you have listed every fact once, stop.

Example transcript:
Marco: I've got the chiropractor on Monday at 8.
Anna: Ugh, it's so loud outside.
Giulia: Domani ritiro le tende dal sarto.
Gino: Ha, brilliant.
Anna: Is the post here yet?

Example reply:
{"memories": [{"who": "Marco", "fact": "Marco has the chiropractor on Monday at 8."}, {"who": "Giulia", "fact": "Giulia ritira le tende dal sarto domani."}]}

Reply with JSON only, in exactly that shape. If the transcript holds nothing worth keeping, reply {"memories": []}.
