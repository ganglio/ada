# No Memories form from a child's addressed speech unless the Owner enables it

A child living in the home is a Housemate, recognised and answered like any other. But a child cannot consent to being listened to, and may say things to Ada that a parent would not expect her to keep. By default the Monologue forms no Memories from a child's addressed speech, and the child's Segments expire like everyone else's. The Owner can turn memory on for a specific child. The cost is that Ada knows less about the children she is meant to grow up alongside, until the Owner opts in.
