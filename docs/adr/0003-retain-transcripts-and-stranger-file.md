# Transcripts are retained briefly, Strangers included

Ada cannot learn who recurring visitors are, or build a Household map from unfiltered speech, if she keeps only pre-judged "meaningful" transcripts. This supersedes the retention clause of ADR 0002: recognised voices' transcripts wait in the Backlog for the Monologue to consolidate (expiring after 48 hours), and Stranger transcripts and provisional Voiceprints sit in the Stranger file for 14 days to allow identification. Raw audio is still never written to disk, and nothing is kept past expiry unless the Owner extends it or the Monologue turns it into a Memory.

## Consequences

Guests who never agreed to be heard have their words and voice held for up to 14 days. This is the price of learning who a recurring visitor is. Only the Owner can name a Stranger or promote them to Friend.
